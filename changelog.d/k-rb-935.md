### Security

- **Every website serves its fonts and Mermaid only as the blueprint pins them:** the fonts, Roboto and Roboto Mono, come from the stylesheet of the blueprint, `.github/mkdocs-blueprint-fonts.css`, and no longer from the stylesheet of Google Fonts, which may change at any time, and the build checks each font and the release of Mermaid against its sha256 in `.github/mkdocs-blueprint-pins.toml`, as it downloads it and in the cache of the week; a file that differs fails the build and never reaches the website. A website downloads Mermaid only when one of its pages still has a `mermaid` block, so the websites of today ask unpkg.com and Google Fonts nothing at all, and Renovate moves Mermaid together with its sha256. A project that names fonts of its own in `theme.font` gets them from Google Fonts, as before ([decision 0164](https://github.com/Dennis-Otto/repo-blueprint/blob/main/docs/decisions/0164-every-website-serves-its-fonts-and-mermaid-only-by-their-sha256.md)).

### Bug fixes

- **The folder of the dashboard has its `sitemap.xml`:** the Dashboard workflow copies the sitemap of the website, with the dashboard in it, into `dashboard/`, as the hooks copy it into every folder of pages, so that a page that names the feed of the releases in its head no longer gets a 404 for `dashboard/sitemap.xml` as it loads.
