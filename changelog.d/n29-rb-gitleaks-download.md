### Bug fixes

- **The secret scan no longer fails in an outage of GitHub's downloads, and works in a repository of an organization:** the check *gitleaks* ran `gitleaks/gitleaks-action`, which downloaded gitleaks from the page of its release without a token, the kind of download that failed for hours on 8 November 2026, at a version a year old that nothing kept current, and which asks every repository of an organization for a license key. It now runs gitleaks 8.30.1 of its release, downloaded through GitHub's API and checked by its sha256, which Renovate moves with each new version. It scans every commit of a pull request, also after the 30th, where the action stopped, and the whole history when the pull request changes gitleaks or its rules in `.gitleaks.toml` or `.gitleaksignore`. Each finding shows as an annotation at its line and in a table of the summary, with the fingerprint that `.gitleaksignore` takes. A newer gitleaks knows more kinds of secrets: when the update of the blueprint finds one in your history, revoke it, or, for a false alarm, add its fingerprint to `.gitleaksignore` ([#806](https://github.com/Dennis-Otto/repo-blueprint/issues/806)).
