### Bug fixes

- The check *diagrams* no longer promises a commit of the diagram bot "in a minute": it says that the bot commits once a runner of *After the checks* is free, and that the bot says on the pull request when it can't. In a repository that is a fork itself, it promises no bot at all. A diagram bot that lacks the private key of the release app now says so in a comment on the pull request instead of leaving it red without a word, and the troubleshooting page says what to do when no commit of the bot comes.
