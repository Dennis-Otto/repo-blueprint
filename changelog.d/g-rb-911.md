### Bug fixes

- An outage of the security advisories of packagist.org that lasts a minute or two no longer fails the checks of a PHP project: after a network error, `composer audit` now tries again 5, 15, 30 and then 60 seconds later, nearly two minutes in all instead of 15 seconds. The audit stays as strict: a vulnerable or abandoned package still fails at once, and when Packagist stays out of reach even longer, the step still fails and says so.
