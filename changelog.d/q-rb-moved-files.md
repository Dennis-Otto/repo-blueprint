### Features

- A release that removes a file of the blueprint no longer leaves your own links to it broken without a word. The update now finds the lines of your project that still name such a file, such as the badge of a workflow in the README, by its path and by the address of the workflow's page and badge. In a page of Markdown, the step *Files of the project* fails until `python3 .github/blueprint.py migrate --fix` points the line at the file that took its place; the fix bot commits that onto the update, and the words of the line stay yours. Every other line, such as a comment under `.github/`, is a warning and a step of `python3 .github/blueprint.py checklist`, and fails nothing. A link to a tag, a commit or another repository stays as it is, and so do `CHANGELOG.md`, `changelog.d/` and `docs/decisions/`. A line that keeps the old name on purpose says `blueprint: keep` in a comment. This release only teaches the fix bot that it may commit such a page; the next one brings the first entries, the workflows *Secret scan* and *Dependency review* that *Quick checks* replaced in 1.22.0.
