### Bug fixes

- The taint analysis of a Nextcloud app follows a path from a request into the storage of a folder as well: `.github/psalm/NextcloudTaintPlugin.php` marks the paths of `IStorage`, the one that `Node::getStorage()` returns, as sinks, where each method reads, writes, lists, moves or removes what is at a path or tells whether it is there (such as `fopen`, `file_put_contents`, `rename`, `unlink` and `mkdir`), and of `writeStream` of `IWriteStreamStorage`. A path that reaches the storage unchecked could climb past the folder with `../` without the analysis noticing; the canary proves the new sinks in every run. The update can fail `check (php)` where such a path comes from input that no function of your app escapes: validate it there, and mark the function that makes it safe with `@psalm-taint-escape file` and a comment on why.
