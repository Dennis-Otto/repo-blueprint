### Features

- **Every release of the issue assistant reaches every repository by itself:** its four workflows and its firewall policy are templates of its release, with which the check `issue-assistant` compares the workflows, so a new release took a person who ran `install` in the blueprint and moved every pin, and its fixes waited for days. Now the new sync bot of the blueprint takes the latest release of the issue assistant every morning, verifies that its release workflow made it (immutable, attested by GitHub, on its `main`, its tag signed with gitsign) and refuses any other, and any release older than the one it has, installs it in the blueprint and keeps one pull request for it, which merges itself once every check passes; the next release of the blueprint brings the release to your repository. Renovate no longer moves the pin of the issue assistant on its own, which failed the check, and actionlint ignores the `queue` of the concurrency group in `issue-assistant.yml`, which it doesn't know yet. `config.toml` of the issue assistant shows its limits of the AI runs per day, per reporter and per closed issue, with their defaults, and `SUPPORT.md` tells reporters that a busy day can leave their issue to the maintainer without an analysis, and that only the duplicate of an issue that the maintainer opened or triaged closes by itself.
