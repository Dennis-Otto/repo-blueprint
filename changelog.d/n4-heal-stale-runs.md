### Features

- **The runs of a replaced commit give their runner back:** a run of a check only tells about its commit, yet once a newer push replaced that commit, or its pull request merged, it still waited for a runner of the account, up to an hour on a busy day. The job *signed commits* on every new commit and the branch bot on every merge now cancel the checks whose commit no open pull request has as its head, with no job of their own, and *After the checks* starts no job for a replaced commit. They never touch the head of a pull request, `main`, a release or a run that someone started again. On 1 November 2026, across seven repositories, that would have cancelled 520 of 547 such runs. `python3 .github/blueprint.py stale-runs --dry-run` shows what it would cancel now.
