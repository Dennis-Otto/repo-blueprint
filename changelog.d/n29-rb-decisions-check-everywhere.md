### Features

- **No two records of a decision with one number:** the new step *Records of decisions* of `scripts/check.sh` fails when two records in `docs/decisions/` share a number, when the numbers skip one, or when the index `docs/decisions/README.md` lists a record twice, misses one, names a file that doesn't exist or lists the records out of order. Two pull requests that each took the next free number used to reach `main` together; now the second one fails once `main` is merged into it, and the log names the next free number for its record. `python3 .github/blueprint.py decisions --fix` fixes what only the index gets wrong, such as a missing row or the order of the rows, and the fix bot commits that onto a pull request. The step passes when a repository has no records.
