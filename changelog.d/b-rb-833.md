### Features

- A repository that the Rollout of the blueprint doesn't start, such as one of another owner, gets a new release of the blueprint within a day instead of a week: the nightly look of the bot-health bot starts the blueprint bot when the blueprint has a newer release and the pull request of the bot doesn't bring it yet, without a run of its own.
- A Home Assistant integration is loaded on the oldest Home Assistant that its `hacs.json` promises: the check `home-assistant` imports every module of `custom_components/` in the image of that release, after installing the requirements of the manifest, and fails at each module that needs a newer Home Assistant.
