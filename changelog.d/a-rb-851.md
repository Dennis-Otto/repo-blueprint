### Features

- **A warning two weeks before an accepted advisory ends:** an entry of an `osv-scanner.toml` accepts advisories until a date, `effectiveUntil` of `[[PackageOverrides]]` or `ignoreUntil` of `[[IgnoredVulns]]`, and from that day on OSV-Scanner reports them again, on `main` and in every pull request that changes a lock file. Nothing looked at how near that date was. Now the job *dependabot alerts* of the OSV-Scanner workflow, on `main` and in its weekly run, warns of each acceptance that ends within 14 days and opens the issue *An acceptance of advisories in osv-scanner.toml ends soon*, which names the file, the package, the version and the date and says what to do: take the update that moves the version, or look at the advisories again and move the date. It keeps the issue current and closes it once none ends that soon; `blueprint.py advisories`, the step *Accepted advisories* of `scripts/check.sh`, names such an acceptance as well, without failing. The job needs `issues: write` for it ([#851](https://github.com/Dennis-Otto/repo-blueprint/issues/851)).
- **A Home Assistant integration accepts the Pillow of its test tools until 2026-12-20:** the test plugin still pins Home Assistant 2026.10 and with it Pillow 12.3.0, so the acceptance, whose end on 2026-11-22 would have turned the checks red, moves on after a new look at its advisories. An existing integration has its own `osv-scanner.toml`, which the update leaves alone: move its `effectiveUntil` for `pillow` 12.3.0 to `2026-12-20` the same way and run `python3 .github/blueprint.py advisories --fix`.
