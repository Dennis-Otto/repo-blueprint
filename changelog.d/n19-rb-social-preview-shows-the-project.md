### Features

- **A new repository's social preview shows the project, not a list of checks:** the card on the right of `.github/social-preview.html` was the same four lines of checks in every repository. It now draws what a project of its kind shows, made from the answers of `copier copy`: the step `uses: owner/name@v1` of an action in a workflow, the `pip install` or `npm install` of a package, the `docker run` of an image, a request to a Worker and its answer, the popup of an extension in the browser's toolbar, the app in the navigation of Nextcloud, the entities of a Home Assistant integration, or the start of the README ([#712](https://github.com/Dennis-Otto/repo-blueprint/issues/712)). The panel is marked `data-default-panel`, and `python3 .github/blueprint.py checklist` has the step *Social preview shows the project* until the project replaces it with a screenshot of itself or a faithful drawing of what it produces. In an existing repository, `.github/social-preview.html` is your own, so an update leaves it alone, and the checklist step finds the old card of checks there too: copy the new panel from a new copy of the blueprint or design your own, render the picture with `bash scripts/social-preview.sh` and upload `.github/social-preview.png` under *Settings → General → Social preview*.
