### Features

- A team can require approving reviews and the review of the code owners for `main`: `required_approvals` and `code_owner_review` of `[branch]` in `.github/repository.project.toml` make the ruleset *Reviews of main*, which the settings bot keeps. The green pull requests of Renovate, Dependabot and the bots still merge by themselves: the branch bot merges them past the review as the release app, once their required checks passed, while every change of a person waits for its approvals.
