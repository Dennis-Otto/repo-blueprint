### Bug fixes

- **The accessibility check checks a page that leads to another address, not the 404 behind it:** a page of an old address that leads elsewhere at once, by its refresh or its script, such as one that leads to a page of another project website, is checked as it shows before it leaves, with its own language and title, and the summary names where it leads. Until now the check followed it to the address in the folder of the website, which has no such page, and checked an empty 404 instead, so it found `document-title` and `html-has-lang` there and nothing of the page itself ([#706](https://github.com/Dennis-Otto/repo-blueprint/issues/706)). Entries of `.github/a11y-accepted.toml` that accept these two rules on such pages now show as no longer found, which warns and fails nothing: take them out, so that a real violation on those pages can't pass unseen. A page that leads elsewhere without a `lang` or a `<title>` of its own fails now, as any other page does.
- **The accessibility check is quicker, and a page can't pass on a scheme that another page saved:** each page opens in its four browsers at the same time, and the pages take turns in tabs that stay open, so a pull request that changes a few pages has its result sooner. Each tab has a browser of its own, so a page that switches its scheme with its toggle can no longer make another page show that scheme while it is checked, and a page that shows its scheme already passes at once, however busy the runner is.
