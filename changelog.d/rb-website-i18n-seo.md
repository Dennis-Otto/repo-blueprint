### Bug fixes

- **The search of a website finds the pages of the reader's language, and search engines see each page in its language:** a website in English and German had one search index for both, so an English search found German pages, often first, a German one the English version of a page that has a German one, sections whose page the i18n plugin had taken out of the index led to `…/undefined`, and the sources of the diagrams showed as the text of a result. Now the hook of the website writes an index for each language, `search/search_index.en.json` and `search/search_index.de.json`, and each page searches its language: a German page the German pages and the English ones that have no German version, at their address in `de/`; on the website of the owner, which adds the search of every project website, `ha-autodarts/de/…` counts as German too. The diagrams, the template of the decision records and any page with `robots: noindex` in its front matter stay out of the search, and the template and such pages out of the sitemap and search engines. A page that the German website shows in English, untranslated, says that its text is English, names the English page as its canonical address and asks search engines to leave it out, and the English page names no German version; every other page names its versions with their full addresses and `x-default`. Each page gets the start of its first paragraph as its description, unless its front matter has `description:`, a page that includes files with `--8<--`, such as the changelog, the date of the newest of them, the sitemap the date of each page's last change instead of that of the build, and the German pages German names for the switch between light and dark. The home page's preview card names the website instead of *Home*. The status badges of a README no longer show on the website, which showed them as the build downloaded them; buttons such as *Open in HACS* stay. A website needs no change of its own: the shared settings now give the i18n plugin `reconfigure_search: false`, which keeps every entry of the search in the index.
