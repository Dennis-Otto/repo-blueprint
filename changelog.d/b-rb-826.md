### Bug fixes

- **A link to a website previews with the designed picture of its repository:** the home page of each language of a website made from the blueprint named a plain card of Material as its picture for chats and social networks, with the name of the website twice and its description cut off. It now names the repository's `.github/social-preview.png`, the picture that GitHub shows for a link to the repository, which the website serves; the inner pages keep the cards with their own titles. `blueprint.py site-check` fails when a page names a picture of the website for its card that the website doesn't have ([#829](https://github.com/Dennis-Otto/repo-blueprint/issues/829)).
- **The description of the blueprint names all of its kinds of projects:** the About box on GitHub, the description of the website and its link previews named six kinds, without Chrome extensions and Cloudflare Workers, and the topics of the repository now include `chrome-extension` and `cloudflare-workers` ([#828](https://github.com/Dennis-Otto/repo-blueprint/issues/828)).

### Features

- **The check of the pages warns of a wall of text:** `blueprint.py pages`, the step *Pages in one column* of `scripts/check.sh`, warns of a paragraph or an item of a list with more than 150 words, which is several screens of a phone without a break, at its line and as an annotation in the CI, next to the warning of a long cell of a table; the records of `docs/decisions/` keep their words, and the warning fails nothing. The pages of the website and `CONTRIBUTING.md` that held up to 660 words in one block, and the `CONTRIBUTING.md` that a new repository starts with, now give each such block a short lead with a list below it ([#826](https://github.com/Dennis-Otto/repo-blueprint/issues/826)).
