### Performance

- **Every repository runs its scheduled workflows at times of its own:** the weekly jobs of all the repositories of an owner no longer start in the same minutes and wait for the same 20 runners of GitHub Free, as the mutation tests of five repositories did on a Saturday, while the pull requests of every repository waited behind them. The blueprint keeps its times, each repository of `.github/fleet.toml` takes its place in the Rollout as its slot and any other one a slot from the hash of its name; each weekly schedule moves by as many days and every schedule by 8 minutes for each, within its hour, so the heavy weekly jobs of the blueprint and the first six repositories of the registry never run in the same hour of a day. The weekly run of the mutation tests takes two runners at once, a pull request still four, and `CONTRIBUTING.md` names the day of the mutation tests of the repository (decision 0082).
