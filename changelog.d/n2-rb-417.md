### Bug fixes

- **A GIF with a dark variant shows one video, as wide as the GIF:** on a website, a `<picture>` with a GIF for each theme and an MP4 beside each showed both videos, one above the other, in both themes, and every video filled the column, also a narrow one such as a popup. Now only the video of the reader's theme shows and plays, and a video is as wide as the GIF, or as the `width` that its `<img>` names, and never wider than the column. A GIF narrower than the column therefore no longer fills it; give its `<img>` a `width` for another size. A project can delete the styles of its own that worked around this.
