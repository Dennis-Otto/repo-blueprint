### Bug fixes

- A new Dependabot alert of a version that an `osv-scanner.toml` accepts closes within a day: the job *dependabot alerts* now runs in the Release workflow, after every push to `main` and every day, instead of only after a push and on Mondays in the OSV-Scanner workflow, and the warning of an acceptance that ends soon comes every day as well. No new schedule. To see what the job would do, run `python3 .github/blueprint.py advisories --dependabot --dry-run`; the OSV-Scanner workflow no longer has the input `dry_run`.
