### Features

- **A website can have several further languages:** a website with `/de/`, `/nl/`, `/fr/` and `/es/` now works as one with German alone does. A page that a further language shows untranslated names no versions in its head, also when another language has translated the page, so `blueprint.py site-check` no longer rejects it, and a project can delete the hook of its own that strips these links. The report of a pull request links `docs/faq.nl.md` at `<site>/nl/faq.html`, for every locale of the i18n plugin in `mkdocs.yml`, and every other changed page at its real address too, `<site>/faq.html` where `<site>/faq/` led nowhere, unless `mkdocs.yml` sets `use_directory_urls: true`. The hints at the style (Vale) and the spelling (cspell) leave out a page in a language that they can't read, such as `docs/faq.nl.md`.
