### Bug fixes

- **The taint analysis of a Nextcloud app runs on Psalm 7:** the plugin that teaches Psalm the controllers, files and HTTP client of Nextcloud used a class that Psalm 7 no longer has, so Psalm 7 stopped with `Class "Psalm\Type\TaintKindGroup" not found`, and the update of Renovate to Psalm 7 stayed red. The plugin now works with Psalm 6 and Psalm 7, which holds the kinds of taint as the bits of an integer: an app keeps the Psalm of its own `composer.json` and moves to Psalm 7 when it merges that update, which goes green once the app has taken this release. A new app starts on Psalm 7 ([#816](https://github.com/Dennis-Otto/repo-blueprint/issues/816)).
