### Bug fixes

- **Every bot that Harden-Runner stops runs again by itself:** a job that blocks stops when Harden-Runner has no agent that blocks, as in GitHub's outage of 8 November 2026, and a bot runs it again once. Some workflows were left out: the bots of pull requests and comments, *Area labels*, *Welcome*, *Dependabot maintenance* and *Commands*, and the workflows that keep an issue of their own, *Diagrams*, *Newest Node* and *Newest Python*. A new pull request then got no welcome, `/fix` got no answer, and the issue of the newest Node waited a week. The bot-health bot now runs their failed jobs once more when Harden-Runner stopped one of them at the first attempt, also in a pull request of a fork, and opens no issue for them; any other failure stays as it was. *Mutation*, which gets no second try, now gets one instead of an issue when Harden-Runner stopped each of its jobs that failed ([#810](https://github.com/Dennis-Otto/repo-blueprint/issues/810)).
