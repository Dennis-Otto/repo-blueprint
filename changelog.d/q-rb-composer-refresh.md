### Features

- The weekly refresh of `composer.lock` merges by itself once every check passes, like every other dependency update, instead of waiting for your review on `renovate/review-lock-file-maintenance`. Know the price: Composer has no cooldown, and whoever publishes a version sets its time in `composer.lock`, so nothing dates it, and the refresh brings versions of any age, also one published minutes before, without the week that every other update waits. The check dependency-review names each version of Composer that the refresh adds as one that nothing dates, and still holds a refresh with a version of npm or PyPI younger than a week. After the update, the next run of Renovate closes a refresh that still waits under the old name as `- autoclosed` and builds it anew on `renovate/composer-lock-file-maintenance` on the next Monday; one whose auto-merge you turned on may merge before that. The refresh of any other package manager, such as a `poetry.lock` or a `Gemfile.lock`, keeps waiting for you (decision 0174).
