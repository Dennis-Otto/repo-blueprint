### Bug fixes

- **Every image gets its new versions again:** no registry tells Renovate when a version of an image was released (ghcr.io gives no date, and Docker Hub none to a request without a login for an image of more than 1,000 tags, such as nextcloud, postgres, python or redis), and Renovate holds an update without a date for good. So Paperless-ngx stayed at 3.3.1 in both Nextcloud apps while 3.4.2 was out, nextcloud and redis stayed at older patch releases, and the Renovate of the Renovate workflow stayed at 44.145.1. An image now comes at the next Monday run without the week of waiting, which the checks of its pull request and its digest stand in for, and the new digest of a tag that stays comes in a pull request of its own, *update the digests of the images*, also beside a group of the project such as Paperless-ngx: in one branch, Renovate dropped one of the two updates of an image. Each update that Renovate still drops that way is now listed in the issue *Renovate logs problems*, with the image, the update that it kept and the one that it dropped (decision 0148, [#859](https://github.com/Dennis-Otto/repo-blueprint/issues/859)).
