### Features

- **The dev container runs ShellCheck too:** no image of a dev container has ShellCheck, so `bash scripts/check.sh` skipped the step *Shell scripts* there with `shellcheck is not installed here; the CI runs it.`, and its findings showed up only in the CI. `.devcontainer/setup.sh` of every kind of project now installs it as the container is created: `shellcheck-py` from the new `.devcontainer/shellcheck-requirements.txt`, pinned with its hashes and kept current by Renovate, in a virtual environment of its own outside the checkout, whose pins never meet those of the project, with only its binary in `/usr/local/bin`. It works on x86-64 and on Arm, as in Docker on a Mac with Apple silicon, where pip builds it from its hash-pinned source: the binary of ShellCheck is checked by its sha256 there too, but the build tools setuptools and setuptools-download come without hashes. The images without Python first get its module `venv`, as they did for the tools of the website. A dev container that exists already gets ShellCheck once it is rebuilt ([#801](https://github.com/Dennis-Otto/repo-blueprint/issues/801)).
