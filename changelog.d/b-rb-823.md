### Features

- **A drop of the mutation score of a Nextcloud app opens the issue too:** the weekly mutation tests of a Nextcloud app now compare the score of Infection with the best of the runs before, as those of Python do. When it falls, the issue *Mutation score dropped* opens with the scores before and now and the files that gained escaped mutants, and it closes once a run is back. Before, a drop showed only as a number in the summary of the run ([#823](https://github.com/Dennis-Otto/repo-blueprint/issues/823)).
- **The translations of an integration, a Nextcloud app or an extension are checked:** a new step *Translations* of `scripts/check.sh` fails when a language lacks a text of its source, keeps a stale one, has a text without words or loses a placeholder such as `{name}`, `%s` or `$NAME$`, when a plural of Nextcloud has the wrong number of forms, or when `l10n/<language>.js` says something else than its `.json`. `blueprint.py translations --fix`, which the fix bot runs, fixes the order, the stale texts and the copies; the repositories can drop the rules that their own checkers share with it ([#832](https://github.com/Dennis-Otto/repo-blueprint/issues/832)).
