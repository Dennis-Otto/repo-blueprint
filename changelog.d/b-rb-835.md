### Features

- **A classic branch protection of `main` no longer goes unnoticed:** an existing repository that takes the blueprint may still have the branch protection of before, which requires the old names of the checks or an approval and keeps every pull request of the bots waiting, while the rulesets look right. `blueprint.py settings check` and `settings apply` now name it with what it requires and the command that removes it, and the checklist shows it as a step of its own. The settings never remove it themselves, as a person may have set it on purpose; the guide and *A required check never reports* of the troubleshooting say how it goes ([#836](https://github.com/Dennis-Otto/repo-blueprint/issues/836)).
