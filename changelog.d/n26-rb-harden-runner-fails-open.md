### Bug fixes

- **A job no longer runs without its block when Harden-Runner can't start it:** when Harden-Runner couldn't download its agent, as in GitHub's outage of 8 November 2026, it went on without it, and a job that should block every host it doesn't need ran with no block at all, with its secrets and tokens, and passed. Every job that blocks now makes sure, right before it waits for its hosts, that the agent of Harden-Runner runs: it waits half a minute at most for one that is still starting, and otherwise stops with the annotation *Harden-Runner doesn't block*, which names the cause, such as an outage of GitHub, and says that it is no problem of your change ([#754](https://github.com/Dennis-Otto/repo-blueprint/issues/754)). The flaky-test bot runs such a test once more, the bot-health bot such a bot, and the bot for failed releases the release; a check of a pull request, such as its title or CodeQL, runs again with your next push or when you run it again. A workflow of your own with a job that blocks copies the step *Make sure that Harden-Runner blocks* from `.github/workflows/welcome.yml`, right before its step *Wait until Harden-Runner lets the hosts through*.
