### Bug fixes

- The bots after the checks no longer run the failed jobs of an older commit of a pull request again after a newer push replaced it, which cancelled the checks of the newest commit although nothing had failed there: the flaky-test bot and the bot for stopped checks ask GitHub for the head of the pull request, or of the branch, just before the rerun, and leave the run of a replaced commit, or of a closed pull request, as it is.
