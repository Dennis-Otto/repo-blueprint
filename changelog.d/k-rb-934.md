### Features

- A Nextcloud app on Psalm 7 no longer reports the methods that only a controller calls as unused: the plugin in `.github/psalm/` makes every controller an entry point of the search for unused code, as `@psalm-api` does, on Psalm 6 and Psalm 7, because Nextcloud calls the actions of a controller itself. After the update, an app can drop the `@psalm-api` that it put on its controllers for Psalm 7, with the comment on why, as paperless-sync and paperless-unified-search did.
