### Features

- **The steps of the website outlive MkDocs:** the page *Automation*, the sitemaps and `llms.txt` come from `.github/blueprint/website.py`, which the hooks of MkDocs call, and `python3 .github/blueprint.py website automation` and `website finish` take the same steps around a build with another generator, such as Zensical, which the websites move to before 5 May 2027. Every website stays as it is.
