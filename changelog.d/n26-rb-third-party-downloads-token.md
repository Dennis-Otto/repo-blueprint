### Bug fixes

- **The weekly SBOM survives an outage of GitHub's downloads without a token:** the SBOM workflow ran `anchore/sbom-action`, whose installer downloads syft from the page of its release without a token, the kind of download that failed for hours in GitHub's outage of 8 November 2026. It now downloads syft through GitHub's API with the token of the job, as the SBOMs of a release do, checked by the sha256 that the blueprint pins, so Renovate keeps its version current too, where it had stayed at 1.52.0 ([#762](https://github.com/Dennis-Otto/repo-blueprint/issues/762)). The SBOM is still an artifact of the run, under the same name. `anchore/sbom-action` leaves the allowed actions of your repository. Decision 0133 now says, for each action of others, whether it gets the token of the job for its downloads and what would help where it can't, such as for Harden-Runner and gitleaks.
