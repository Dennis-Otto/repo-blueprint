### Bug fixes

- **Every website is accessible in both themes, and a check proves it:** the websites failed WCAG 2.2 AA on every page: the tabs of the header had 4.3:1 to the header, links in the text stood apart by their color alone, buttons with a border had 2.3:1 in the dark theme, some colors of code less than 4.5:1, and the keyboard stopped on links around pictures of the other theme that the page didn't show. The stylesheet of the blueprint, which the hook of the website now serves before a project's own, underlines the links in the text, gives the tabs, the dark links, the buttons and the code enough contrast, and hides those links. A picture for each theme, a `<picture>` with a dark `<source>`, now follows the switch of the website rather than the theme of the system and loads only in its theme, and the lightbox shows only the pictures of the reader's theme; the theme of the system is the first choice of the switch, so a change of it shows on the next page; a website whose `mkdocs.yml` sets its own `palette` gets this by putting an entry with `media: "(prefers-color-scheme)"` first, as `.github/mkdocs-blueprint.yml` does. After each build, the check *docs* opens every page in Chrome, in the light and the dark theme, runs axe-core on it and names each serious or critical violation in its summary and as an annotation at its page, with a link that explains the fix. For now it only reports; a later release makes it fail the check, so fix what it names on your website now. On your machine, `npm ci --prefix .github/a11y --ignore-scripts && node .github/a11y/check.mjs site` runs it after a build.

### Performance

- **Websites load a fraction of what they did on a phone:** each picture loads as the reader comes to it, with its width and height so that the page doesn't shift, and the first one of a page first. A GIF with an MP4 of the same name beside it plays on the website as that video, while it is in view, with a WebP or PNG of the same name until it plays; GitHub keeps the GIF. The release of Mermaid that draws the diagrams is now fixed, so that two builds of the same commit draw them alike, and Renovate moves it. On a phone with 1.6 Mbit/s, the home page of the blueprint loads 1.6 MB instead of 3.5 MB, and its largest picture appears after 1.7 seconds instead of 7.5. The recording of `copier copy` in the README is less than half as large, and the picture bot now also renews videos.
