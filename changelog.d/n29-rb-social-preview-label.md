### Bug fixes

- **The reminder to upload a new social preview no longer counts as a failure:** the issue *Upload the new social preview*, which the bot-health bot keeps open until GitHub shows the committed picture, had the label `bot-failure`, although nothing fails there and only a person can upload the picture. It now has the label `manual-step`, for a step of a bot that waits for a person, which the bot creates in a repository whose `.github/labels.toml` lacks it; an issue opened before keeps its labels until the upload closes it. A new repository gets the label from its `.github/labels.toml`; an existing one adds it there with the lines of `template/.github/labels.toml.jinja`, or leaves it to the bot ([#807](https://github.com/Dennis-Otto/repo-blueprint/issues/807)).
