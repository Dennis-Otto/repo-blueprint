### Features

- **The issue assistant of a Chrome extension and of a Cloudflare Worker may link to the documentation of its kind:** both kinds have the stack `node`, so `.github/issue-assistant/config.toml` let their assistant link only to `nodejs.org` and `www.npmjs.com`, and a link to the pages that a reporter needs was taken out of its comment. A Chrome extension now gets `developer.chrome.com` and `chromewebstore.google.com`, a Cloudflare Worker `developers.cloudflare.com`; the next update brings them to an existing repository whose list of hosts is still the blueprint's, and the hosts of every other kind stay as they are ([#848](https://github.com/Dennis-Otto/repo-blueprint/issues/848)).
