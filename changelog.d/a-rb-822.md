### Bug fixes

- **Bot health gets its own second try after a stop of Harden-Runner:** when Harden-Runner stopped a job of *Bot health* itself, about one run in 85, the end of the bot that it looked at went unseen until the nightly look, and the failed run of *Bot health* stayed on `main` until a person ran it again. Nothing can follow the end of *Bot health*, so its nightly look now runs the failed jobs once more of its own runs of the last two days that Harden-Runner stopped at their first attempt: the end of the bot is looked at again, its second try or its issue comes, and the run of *Bot health* turns green ([#822](https://github.com/Dennis-Otto/repo-blueprint/issues/822)).
