### Bug fixes

- **The check of the website names every picture that a page lost:** the websites of issue-assistant and homedarts-for-autodarts showed an empty paragraph in place of the screenshots of the README, such as the first analysis, the dry run and the findings of issue-assistant and ten pictures of the home page of homedarts-for-autodarts, and paperless-sync had two such gaps until it took 1.0.8. The lightbox plugin took them out with selectolax 1.0.0 and 1.0.1, and the check *docs* passed. 1.0.8 already brought selectolax 1.0.2, which keeps them, so issue-assistant and homedarts-for-autodarts show them again once they take 1.0.8 or later. Now `blueprint.py site-check` also reads the Markdown of every page in `docs_dir` of `mkdocs.yml`, with the parts of the README that it includes from the `base_path` of the snippets, and fails when the built page lacks one of its pictures or has an empty paragraph where one was, with the page and the picture. A picture may show as the website has it: from the checkout, for each scheme, as a video, downloaded from another server or in the reader's language; a badge, which the website leaves out, needs to show nowhere. The build itself now names the files of the pictures that a plugin took out, not only their number. With `--docs`, the command reads the pages from another folder. A row of badges in a centered paragraph of HTML, `<p align="center">`, now leaves no empty paragraph on the website, and a spacer `&nbsp;` counts as text.
