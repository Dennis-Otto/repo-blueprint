### Bug fixes

- The blueprint bot no longer fails on a large update with GitHub's 422 "request timed out": it sends the files of its commit in steps of up to 100 kB, asks again for a step that GitHub timed out, and when GitHub keeps failing, it commits the update through `createCommitOnBranch` and lists the modes of files that this commit lacks. The Rollout of the blueprint now waits for the second try that Bot health gives a failed blueprint bot, and reports a failure only when that try fails too.
