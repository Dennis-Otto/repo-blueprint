### Bug fixes

- **The link check no longer leaves out a document only for a word in its name:** it left out each document whose path merely held the name of a folder that it skips, such as `docs/decisions/0000-template.md` of every repository for the blueprint's `template/`, a decision with `build` or `vendor` in its name, or `.github/pull_request_template.md`, so a broken link there never failed a check. Now it leaves out only the changelog, the texts of the licenses in `LICENSES/`, the folders of the dependencies and of a build, such as `node_modules/` and `dist/`, and the blueprint's own `template/` ([#749](https://github.com/Dennis-Otto/repo-blueprint/issues/749)). The first run of the link check after the update reads the documents that it left out before, and a link there that is broken fails it until you fix the link or list it in `.lycheeignore` with its reason.
