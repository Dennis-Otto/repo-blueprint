### Bug fixes

- **Every bot names what its release app lacks, rather than fail at its token:** in a repository that names no release app yet, or has no private key for it, the settings bot, the upstream bot of a Nextcloud app and the other bots failed at the token with an error that named only a missing input. Each bot that acts as the release app now checks first, in its step *Check that the release app is set up*, and says in an annotation and its summary whether the variable `RELEASE_AUTOMATION_CLIENT_ID` or the secret `RELEASE_AUTOMATION_PRIVATE_KEY` is missing, and that `python3 .github/blueprint.py checklist` sets it up. A bot whose work is what the app does, such as the blueprint bot, the settings bot or Renovate, then fails; one that only helps a pull request along, such as the fix bot, the branch bot or the merge of Dependabot's updates, warns and goes on without it. Decision 0063 says why.
