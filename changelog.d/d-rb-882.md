### Bug fixes

- **A changed picture comes with one comparison of the same frame, which a phone shows well:** the picture of the difference of an animation showed the frame with the most changes, while the pictures before and after beside it showed the first frame, so it looked like another picture, and its pale gray copy of the page left hardly anything to recognize. `screenshots keep` now also writes, for each changed picture, the place of the change before and after in one picture at `comparison/<path>` of the artifact: cut to the changes with room around them, in the colors of the pictures and at their size, so that their text stays readable, side by side or one above the other for a wide place, with the same red frame around each change on both sides. The report names it as `comparison`, with `frame`, the number of the frame it shows, and `frames`, how many the animation has, and the table of the screenshot bot's pull request names the frame of an animation. The difference beside it shows the picture in its own colors behind a thin veil of its own average color, so that a dark page stays dark, with each changed pixel in red (decision 0149, [#882](https://github.com/Dennis-Otto/repo-blueprint/issues/882)).
